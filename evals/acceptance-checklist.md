# Acceptance checklist

- [ ] The topic is famous, visually interesting, evidence-based, and not previously produced.
- [ ] Research supports all dates, architecture, clothing, tools, technology, and events shown.
- [ ] Narration is approximately 150–190 words and follows a clear chronological structure.
- [ ] Ethan Sterling is used unless the user explicitly requested another voice.
- [ ] CloneVoice narration reached Completed status before VideoExpress scene production continued.
- [ ] The exact completed narration was imported into VideoExpress and its duration was recorded.
- [ ] Landscape 16:9 is active for the project and every generation.
- [ ] Every scene uses Text to Video, Advanced Mode, manual duration, and automatic video-prompt enhancement off.
- [ ] No negative-prompt field was used.
- [ ] Every prompt independently specifies the historical period, location, architecture, materials, equipment, people, costumes, actions, camera movement, and documentary style.
- [ ] Every visible person performs purposeful continuous movement.
- [ ] Clothing, equipment, architecture, and monument geometry remain historically and visually consistent.
- [ ] Every rendered clip was watched and inspected before timeline placement.
- [ ] Rejected or missing clips were regenerated individually.
- [ ] Every required new-project clip exists exactly once and no older-project clip is present.
- [ ] All visual clips have volume set to zero.
- [ ] Visual clips are on the upper track in chronological narration order.
- [ ] Narration is complete and unmodified on the lower audio track.
- [ ] Auto Align was applied and no positive gaps exist between clips.
- [ ] The final visual endpoint matches the narration endpoint exactly; only the final visual clip may be trimmed for a small overrun.
- [ ] The project was saved as `[Historical Topic] — Ethan Sterling` and left open for review.
